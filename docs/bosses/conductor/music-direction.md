# The Conductor — music direction

Status: core direction accepted on 2026-09-10. Composition, arrangement, tempo,
meter map, and final sound palette remain to be designed.

## Accepted direction

The score combines a **mechanical chamber orchestra** with **restrained
deconstructed club**.

- The chamber-orchestral layer supplies the musical identity: conducted strings,
  organ, percussion, and other acoustic or mechanical timbres.
- The deconstructed-club layer supplies fractured rhythm, negative space, metallic
  impacts, abrupt edits, and controlled rhythmic instability.
- The fusion should sound deliberately conducted. Electronic disruption is part of
  The Conductor's authority, not an unrelated layer pasted over an orchestra.
- Brief breakcore-like eruptions may be used at peaks, but constant maximal density
  would undermine readable attacks and The Conductor's precise character.
- Silence, held tension, false releases, and hard cutoffs are musical material and
  may support Fermata, Cutoff and Re-entry, and Cadenza.

## Gameplay relationship

- The authored score and encounter chart share one authoritative musical timeline.
- Conducting preparations, ictus points, attack releases, musical accents, and major
  visual impacts must agree rhythmically.
- Rhythmic fracture may complicate the player's read, but gameplay-critical cues
  remain visible and learnable.
- The score may become erratic without becoming random: recurring motifs and phrase
  transformations should reward pattern recognition.

## Still open

- composition duration and large-scale form;
- base tempo and any tempo regions;
- meter map and polymetric or displaced-accent passages;
- exact acoustic and electronic instrumentation;
- recurring boss motif and Cadenza material;
- which musical events carry each authored combat phrase.
