# Idle–Ictus continuity review 01

Preview: `continuity-lab.html`.

This isolated motion review combines already approved material rather than
authoring a new attack. Its 5.2-second loop is one complete two-second idle
cycle, the 1.2-second Ictus wind-up/stroke/recovery, and a second complete idle
cycle. Ictus begins precisely when the lead idle returns to drawing 1; the
settling idle likewise begins at drawing 1 immediately after recovery.

The review intentionally contains no hazards, player, damage logic or encounter
timing. It exists to expose discontinuities in palm registration, apparent scale,
baton grip, anatomy and the separately rendered baton-tip magic. If either seam
is visibly discontinuous, only the required whole-hand bridge drawings should be
authored. Approved idle and Ictus source images remain unchanged.

The painted-matte isolation used by the idle-only lab now lives in the shared
`idle-sheet.ts` helper so both previews render the exact same cleaned idle art.
