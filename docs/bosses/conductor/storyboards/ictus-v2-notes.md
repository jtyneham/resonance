## Revised gesture

04 baton pointing toward the camera at release → 05 upward withdrawal/rebound.

At04, the baton is foreshortened: its tip sits in front of the grip and most of
the shaft is hidden in depth. It has not physically shortened. Thumb/index retain
the pinch, and the three free fingers curl together. The recoil here is the
gesture's own rebound; player counterattacks still never interrupt the boss.

The new impact pose replaces v1's lower-left strike. In-betweens must carry the
wrist turn and gradual foreshortening so the baton does not pop between lengths.
The game camera remains fixed. The final frame set must preserve palm registration,
consistent scale/anatomy/ornaments and genuine transparency. The red guides and
crescent on the review board are illustrations, not embedded runtime attack art.

Lane-specific aiming variants remain optional ideas, not new approved mechanics.
This update changes the storyboard and design record only; the existing live study
still shows the old rig.
