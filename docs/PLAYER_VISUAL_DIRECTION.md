# Player character — visual direction

This document records the selected player-character concept. It is a design
reference, not an instruction to place the generated concept image directly into
the game as a production sprite.

## Selected direction

The player is the **Broken Resonance Mote** shown in
`docs/references/player-broken-resonance-mote-concept.png`.

- A compact, asymmetrical graphite shell or cracked tuning chamber.
- A warm luminous fissure/core held inside the dark body.
- Two short mismatched upper prongs that subtly evoke a damaged tuning fork.
- A small number of detached shell fragments that support expressive motion.
- Worn blackened material, thin aged-ivory edges, and very restrained accents.
- No face, eyes, humanoid anatomy, conventional music-note silhouette, or ornate
  Conductor-like construction.

The selected concept is the second generated player exploration from 2026-09-10.
Its design direction is accepted; exact proportions, palette balance, sprite
resolution, and production animation pieces remain to be refined.

## Shared character art style

The canonical Conductor reference establishes the broad art style for characters
throughout Resonance:

- Rough, grungy semi-pixel rendering.
- Chunky, intentionally visible pixel edges.
- Dithered shading and worn, handmade surface texture.
- Strong silhouettes against deep black.
- Imperfect visual texture supported by precise animation and gameplay.

Characters share this rendering language without sharing The Conductor's palette,
materials, anatomy, or level of ornament.

## Animation implications

- Grounded: the shell rests in a compact, stable configuration.
- Lateral movement: the body leans and detached shards trail briefly.
- Jump: the shell opens enough to make the airborne state unmistakable.
- Absorption: the hollow wave collapses into the central fissure.
- Charged: restrained orbiting rings show stored resonance.
- Damage: the shell separates abruptly, then re-forms.

## Counterattack requirement

The final counterattack is still open. It must be visually distinct from the
player character and remain readable while travelling toward the boss through a
dense formation.
